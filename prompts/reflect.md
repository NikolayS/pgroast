## Reflection round

You already answered once from your angle. Now you are asked one short question about your own
work, with your earlier answer attached (and, in later rounds, the critic's verdicts on it).
Take the question at face value and look again at the material.

Return only changes:

- `objections`: new objections that your earlier answer missed. Same fields and same rules as
  before: specific to this proposal, concrete failure scenario, evidence from the material
  only, precedents by id only. Do not repeat or rephrase objections you already raised.
- `revised`: earlier objections of yours that you now want to state differently (for example
  after a critic's point that is partly right): give the `objection_id` and the full objection
  again. Use this only for a real change of substance or severity.
- `withdrawn`: earlier objections of yours that you no longer stand behind: give the
  `objection_id` and the `reason`. Withdrawing a weak objection is good work.
- `note`: one or two sentences: what you checked this time.

All three lists may be empty, and that is a valid and common answer: do not manufacture changes
to have something to show. In the first reflection round only `objections` is used.
